Family Travel Guide & Tips
Essential UK and Scotland tips for smooth travels with a toddler.
🚗 UK Driving Guidelines
• Drive on the left side of the road.
• At roundabouts, yield to traffic approaching from your right.
• Scottish single-track roads: Pull into "Passing Places" on your left if a car is approaching.
• UK child seat law: Children under 135 cm or 12 years must use a car seat.
🚌 London Public Transit with Stroller
• Children under 5 travel free on London Underground, buses, and DLR without a ticket.
• Tap in and out using your contactless credit or debit card.
• Waterloo, London Bridge, and Westminster stations offer step-free elevator access for strollers.
• Uber Boat (Thames Clipper) is fully accessible and stroller-friendly.
🩺 Toddler Care & Packing
• Scottish weather shifts quickly: Pack waterproof rain jackets and fleece layers.
• Carry a spare change of clothes to Peppa Pig World for the outdoor splash zone.
• Boots pharmacies are present in every town and airport for pediatric essentials and milk.
🎫 Sky Garden Booking Window
• Free admission tickets are released 3 weeks in advance on Monday mornings.
• For your visit on Friday, Sep 18, reserve your slots on Monday, August 31, 2026.
